Welcome to the sunny and beautiful Island Delfino.

Where the sunsets are beautiful, the water is cool, and the lemonade is positively refreshing.

Island Delfino is home to 100s of the Pianta people, an island of large-bodied bipeds with large hands and noses. They are strong enough to hurl grown men up to a minimum of 100 yards. In fact, it is one of the island's many tourist attractions. Many come to be hurled into the sea.
Come try it! It sure beats walking into the ocean!
